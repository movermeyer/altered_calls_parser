Altered Damage Call Parser

Parse, validate, normalize, and autocomplete Altered LARP damage calls.

Unofficial, community-built project — not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators of Altered LARP.

This parser only tells you whether a call is syntactically valid. Additional rules may apply that make it semantically invalid.

    Typically, a damage call reads: effect, then a number, then a damage type — and every part is optional.

    Try: Knockdown 5 Fire 💥 Daze 1 🫨 3 Stamina Drain 🪫 Full Auto 3 Flesh 🔫 Overwhelm Knockdown 10 10 Stamina Drain Poison 🙈
